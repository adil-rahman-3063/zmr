# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how ZMR Music ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the ZMR Music mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our policies and practices regarding your information and how we will treat it. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, your choice is not to use our App. By accessing or using this App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect several types of information from and about users of our App, including:

### A. Personal Information You Provide to Us
*   **Authentication Data:** When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a unique identifier provided by Google.
*   **Account Information:** Any preferences, settings, playlists, or favorite tracks you explicitly save or sync using our Supabase backend.

### B. Technical and Usage Information (Collected Automatically)
*   **Device Information:** We may collect information about your mobile device, including the device's unique identifier, operating system version, and general hardware specifications.
*   **Usage Logs and Analytics:** Through **Firebase Analytics**, we collect information about how you interact with the App, such as features used, screens visited, playback session lengths, and technical errors or crash logs.
*   **Cookies and Local Storage:** We use local storage (such as `SharedPreferences` on Android) to store playback states, persistent queues, system preferences, settings, and cached session tokens.

### C. Third-Party Data
*   **YouTube API & InnerTube Integration:** To extract and stream audio content, the App interacts with YouTube endpoints. This involves sending anonymous technical requests (e.g., visitor data, browser user-agents, and tokens) to extract media streams. We do not link your Google Account used for app authentication with your YouTube streaming activities.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including to:
*   Provide, operate, and maintain the core functionalities of the App (such as music streaming and playlist management).
*   Authenticate your identity and manage your user account via **Supabase**.
*   Send you system alerts, updates, or notifications via **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**.
*   Monitor and analyze app usage, performance metrics, and crash data to improve the user experience.
*   Enforce our Terms of Service and protect the security of our services.

---

## 3. How Your Information is Shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information under the following circumstances:
*   **Service Providers:** We share data with third-party infrastructure providers that perform services on our behalf:
    *   **Supabase:** For secure user database management and authentication hosting.
    *   **Firebase (Google):** For real-time analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications.
*   **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of ZMR Music, our users, or the public.

---

## 4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

*   **Security:** We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures (such as OAuth 2.0 authentication and encryption of tokens) designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
*   **Retention:** We retain your account data (e.g., profile details, playlist configurations) for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our active production databases in Supabase.
*   **Local Caching:** Audio cache and playback histories reside locally on your device and can be cleared at any time by wiping the App's storage or cache in your device settings.
