# ZMR Release Notes

## Version 2.4.1

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
* **Sleep Timer:** Resolved an issue where the sleep timer would not automatically pause playback when the timer expired.

## Version 2.4

### 🚀 What's New
* **Pick Up Right Where You Left Off!** 
  ZMR now persistently remembers your exact playback state. Even if you completely close the app from your recent apps view, your active queue, current song, shuffle/repeat settings, and exact timestamp are saved. Next time you open the app, your music is ready and waiting for you!

### ✨ Enhancements & Polish
* **Fluid Queue Gestures:** The queue is now seamlessly interactive! You can smoothly drag the queue sheet down to dismiss it. Also, swiping down on the background of the player while the queue is open will smartly close just the queue, rather than collapsing the entire player.
* **Responsive Playback Controls:** Added sleek loading indicators to the Next and Previous buttons in the main player, so you always know when the app is fetching the next track.
* **Immersive Scrolling:** Fixed layout issues on the Artist page so content now scrolls beautifully behind the bottom navigation bar, while still leaving the bottom-most items fully interactive.

### 🐛 Under the Hood
* Extensive internal optimization, linting cleanup, and fixes for a more robust and snappy experience.
